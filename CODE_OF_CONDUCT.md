# Code of Conduct / 社区行为准则

## Our pledge / 我们的承诺

We pledge to make participation in Claude Rescue Reader welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or personal circumstances.

我们承诺为所有参与者提供友好、无骚扰的协作环境，不因经验、身份、背景或个人处境而区别对待。

## Expected behavior / 期望行为

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy. Never post another person's Claude export, conversation title, account data, local path, screenshot, or identifier without explicit permission.
- Use synthetic fixtures and redacted examples whenever possible.
- Accept corrections gracefully and help keep discussions focused on the project.

- 保持尊重、具体和建设性。
- 讨论观点与代码，不攻击个人。
- 尊重隐私。未经明确许可，不得公开他人的 Claude 导出、对话标题、账号数据、本地路径、截图或标识符。
- 尽量使用合成测试数据与脱敏示例。
- 平和接受纠正，并帮助讨论聚焦于项目本身。

## Unacceptable behavior / 不可接受的行为

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized language, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information or attempting to identify users from exported data.
- Deliberately submitting secrets, malware, exploit payloads, or real private archives to issues, pull requests, fixtures, or CI.
- Persistent disruption, spam, or bad-faith misrepresentation of another person's work.

- 骚扰、威胁、歧视、性化言论或人身攻击。
- 公开私人信息，或试图从导出数据中识别用户身份。
- 故意向 Issue、PR、测试数据或 CI 提交密钥、恶意代码、攻击载荷或真实私人档案。
- 持续扰乱讨论、发送垃圾内容，或恶意歪曲他人的工作。

## Scope and enforcement / 适用范围与执行

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, release channels, and public spaces where someone represents the project.

本准则适用于仓库 Issue、Pull Request、代码审查、讨论、发布渠道，以及代表本项目参与的公开场合。

For ordinary moderation concerns, contact the maintainer through the repository without reposting sensitive material. For a confidential report, use GitHub's private vulnerability-reporting channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions that violate this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

普通社区问题可通过仓库联系维护者，但不要重复发布敏感内容。需要保密处理时，请使用 [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) 中说明的 GitHub 私密报告渠道。维护者可以编辑、隐藏、拒绝或删除违反本准则的内容，并可暂时或永久限制参与权限。

Enforcement decisions should be fair, proportionate, and confidential where possible. Retaliation against someone who makes a good-faith report is not permitted.

执行决定应尽量公平、适度并保护隐私。不得报复善意举报者。
